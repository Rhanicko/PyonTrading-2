# Research coordinator

Keep source readers independent and show their status separately. A conclusion must list supporting evidence, conflicts, unavailable inputs and gate outcomes. Missing data, stale data, unknown news risk or unvalidated strategy performance takes precedence over directional commentary.

The coordinator may return `NO_TRADE`, watch or review states only. It cannot create broker orders, override a hard safety gate or claim confidence unsupported by stored evidence.
