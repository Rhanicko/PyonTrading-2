# Economic news watcher

Retain publisher, URL, retrieval time, event time, timezone, currency, impact, forecast, previous, actual and revision when supplied by the source. Calendar absence or stale retrieval means `NEWS_CHECK_UNVERIFIED`, not no risk.

Do not infer BUY or SELL from an economic surprise. Separate reported release facts from observed market reaction and from analytical interpretation.
